After 14 years of military service, I've spent a good portion of my life learning how to be disciplined, adapting to change, traveling to places I never imagined, and finding my way through some pretty unpredictable chapters. I've learned to appreciate the big things in life, but more importantly, I've learned not to overlook the little ones.

I'm only 33, but life has already given me plenty of stories. And somewhere along the way, I realized that growing older doesn't necessarily mean having everything figured out. Sometimes it means learning to slow down, appreciate the quiet moments, and find happiness in the things that make life feel a little more special.

That's part of why I wanted to create this little library.

I wanted to build something that felt like you. A place where your love for books, fantasy, animals, nature, and all the little things that make you who you are could come together. A place that feels warm, peaceful, and a little magical. Somewhere you can escape into another world, discover something new, or simply enjoy being surrounded by the things you love.

Maybe it's a little unconventional to build someone a library website, but I've never believed that meaningful gestures have to be conventional. Sometimes the best way to show someone you care is to pay attention to the things that make them happy even when they think your not paying attention.

I hope this corner of the internet is where you can slow down a little, get lost in a good story, explore, study, and feel maybe feel at home.

Sueloyd, this library is yours.

Not because it has every book in the world, but because it was built around one person who makes the world a little more interesting to explore.

And if nothing else, I hope every time you visit, you remember that someone thought you were worth building an entire little world for.
